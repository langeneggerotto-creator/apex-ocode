# DreamMusicForge Film Science Institute v1.0

Status: CANONICAL-DRAFT

## Mission
Continuously discover, test, falsify, validate, and operationalize reusable principles of audiovisual experience. Software compilers consume promoted knowledge; unverified hypotheses remain quarantined as research.

## Scientific Domains
Perception Science; Emotion Science; Narrative Science; Music Science; Cinematography Science; Lighting Science; Color Science; Editing Science; Performance Science; Audience Science.

## Research Loop
Observe -> Measure -> Analyze -> Hypothesize -> Experiment -> Verify -> Promote -> Learn.

## Truth Boundary
Every research claim carries one of: VERIFIED, MEASURED, INFERRED, POSSIBLE, UNKNOWN. Experimental laws additionally carry evidence references, confidence, counterexamples, scope, and benchmark lineage.

## Example Research Candidate
Progressive Spectacle: escalation in visual complexity may create stronger perceived climax when lower-complexity passages establish contrast. This remains a hypothesis until benchmarked and tested; it must not be encoded as universal fact.

## Benchmark Universe
Music videos; concert films; short films; animation; commercials; documentaries; educational film; performance film; feature-film sequences. References are used to extract general production principles, not to reproduce protected expression.

## Experimental Requirements
Experiments must define question, hypothesis, variables, controls where feasible, metrics, sample, analysis method, result, confidence, failure modes, and promotion recommendation.

## Promotion Rule
No hypothesis enters canonical production logic solely because it appears persuasive. Promotion requires traceable evidence and bounded applicability.
