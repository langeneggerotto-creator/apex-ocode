# DreamMusicForge Studio OS Architecture v3.0

Status: CANONICAL-DRAFT

## North Star
DreamMusicForge is a provider-neutral AI cinematic operating system that compiles human intent into coherent audiovisual experiences. Renderers execute bounded production tasks; DreamMusicForge owns creative intent, continuity, verification, assembly, evidence, and learning.

## Operating Stack
Human Dream -> Transformation Intelligence -> Experience Intelligence -> Creative Intelligence -> Pre-Production -> Production Intelligence -> Rendering -> Verification -> Assembly -> Post Production -> Learning.

## Executive Functions
- Chief Experience function: audience journey, attention, emotion, memory, transformation, replay value.
- Chief Creative function: artistic coherence, originality, symbolism, visual language.
- Chief Production function: feasibility, schedule, budget, task graph, approvals.
- Chief Technical function: architecture, provider adapters, media pipeline, reliability.
- Chief Quality function: gates, rejection, repair, continuity, final acceptance.
- Chief Research function: benchmark extraction, film science, experiments, capability atlas.
- Chief Learning function: evidence promotion, canonical knowledge, Academy integration.

## Creative Divisions
Dream Intelligence; Transformation Intelligence; Experience Compiler; Audience Compiler; Narrative Compiler; Film Genome; Visual DNA; Music DNA; Brand Identity; Motif Compiler; Surprise Engine; Spectacle Planner; Visual Rhythm; Director Intelligence.

## Pre-Production Divisions
Concept Art; Storyboard; Scene Architect; Shot Designer; Stage/World Designer; Costume Studio; Hair & Makeup; Prop Studio; Performance Planner; Choreography; Camera Designer; Lens Language; Lighting Designer; Transition Architect; Emotional Blocking; Silence/Stillness Planner.

Rule: continuity-critical information must be decided before rendering. Rendering may elaborate approved details but must not silently redefine canonical identity, costume topology, world geometry, music position, or narrative intent.

## Production Intelligence
Constraint Solver -> Production Planner -> Shot Planner -> Task Graph -> Video Slicer -> Renderer Planner -> Provider Compiler -> Verification Planner -> Repair Planner -> Assembly Planner.

## Rendering
Kling, Veo, Runway, local models, and future providers are interchangeable execution backends behind provider-neutral contracts.

## Verification Divisions
Technical, Identity, Continuity, Motion, Physics, Music, Lip Sync, Emotion, Narrative, Meaning, Style, Production Value, Audience Attention, Brand, and Final Film QA.

## Assembly / Post
Timeline Editing; Shot Selection; Masking; Compositing; VFX; Transition Design; Color; Lip Sync; Audio Mix; Mastering; Versioning; Distribution Packaging.

## Research + Learning
Benchmark Lab; Capability Lab; Experiment Lab; Human Psychology Lab; Film Science Lab; Discovery Engine; Canonical Knowledge Registry; DreamMusicForge Academy.

## Governing Continuity Model
Pixel continuity, state continuity, causal continuity, and semantic continuity are distinct and must never be conflated.

## Governing Music Model
Audio continuity, musical continuity, vocal continuity, and performance continuity are distinct and must never be conflated.
