# DreamMusicForge Film Science Institute v1.0

## Purpose
Create, test, validate, and govern reusable cinematic knowledge so DreamMusicForge improves from evidence rather than intuition alone.

## Scientific domains
1. Perception Science — saliency, eye trace, change blindness, motion perception, facial recognition, auditory perception.
2. Emotion Science — anticipation, empathy, tension, relief, hope, awe, nostalgia, inspiration.
3. Narrative Science — causality, expectation, surprise, payoff, pacing, memory.
4. Music Science — rhythm, harmony, dynamics, synchronization, expectation, resolution.
5. Cinematography Science — framing, perspective, depth, camera motion, visual weight.
6. Lighting Science — contrast, visibility, atmosphere, attention guidance.
7. Color Science — palette, contrast, harmony, readability, emotional associations.
8. Editing Science — pacing, transition psychology, rhythm, expectation management.
9. Performance Science — gaze, gesture, posture, expression, authenticity, choreography.
10. Audience Science — attention, comprehension, recall, emotional response, replay behavior.

## Film Law lifecycle
Observation -> hypothesis -> experiment -> measurement -> validation -> counterexample review -> confidence assignment -> benchmark replication -> canonical promotion.

Every Film Law record must include:
- law_id
- name
- statement
- evidence
- confidence
- counterexamples
- applicable contexts
- failure conditions
- benchmark_ids
- last_validated
- status: hypothesis | experimental | validated | canonical | deprecated

## Canonical ontology
Dream; Transformation; Audience; Experience; Story; Character; Performer; World; Scene; Shot; Slice; State; Transition; Music; Beat; Lyric; Phoneme; Camera; Lighting; Color; Costume; Prop; Motion; Emotion; Meaning; Verification; Evidence; Renderer; Assembly Task.

## Temporal model
Every time slice may reference absolute time, frame number, beat, bar, lyric, phoneme, scene, shot, performer state, camera state, lighting state, world state, emotional target, audience target, and active motif state.

## State-machine doctrine
Persistent entities are stateful. A state transition must be explicit, permitted, causally plausible, and verifiable.

## Digital Film Twin
The Digital Film Twin is the canonical simulation of intended film state. Renderers approximate the Twin; the Twin does not conform to renderer mistakes. The Twin owns intended performer, world, prop, camera, lighting, music, emotion, and audience-state timelines.

## Experimental framework
Each experiment defines: question, hypothesis, controls, inputs, provider/model version, method, metrics, result, uncertainty, conclusion, reproducibility artifacts, and recommendation.

## Self-evolution loop
Observe -> Measure -> Analyze -> Hypothesize -> Experiment -> Verify -> Promote -> Learn.

No hypothesis becomes production canon solely because it sounds plausible.
