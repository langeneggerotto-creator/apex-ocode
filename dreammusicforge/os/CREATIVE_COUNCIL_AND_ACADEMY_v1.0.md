# DreamMusicForge Creative Council and Academy v1.0

Status: CANONICAL-DRAFT

## Creative Council
The council provides multi-disciplinary review before production promotion. Roles: Dream Architect, Experience Director, Story Director, Music Director, Performance Director, Choreography Director, Camera Director, Lighting Director, Color Director, Production Designer, Editor, Audience Advocate, Film Critic, Production Manager, Renderer Specialist, Quality Director, Research Lead.

Each role emits structured findings, risks, required changes, confidence, and approval state. The Production Strategy Engine reconciles conflicts; no individual agent may silently override canonical invariants.

## Council Gates
Concept Gate -> Pre-Production Gate -> Production Readiness Gate -> Render Acceptance Gate -> Sequence Gate -> Final Film Gate.

## Academy
The DreamMusicForge Academy converts validated research, benchmark analyses, production failures, repairs, and canonical film laws into educational material for humans and AI agents.

Academy functions: benchmark replay, side-by-side production comparisons, Film Genome visualization, failure diagnosis training, renderer capability education, editing/camera/lighting modules, production-law lineage, and guided experiment design.

## Learning Cycle
Research -> validated knowledge -> Academy -> improved human/agent decisions -> better productions -> new evidence -> research.
