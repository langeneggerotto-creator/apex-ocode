# DreamMusicForge / APEX Movie Forge Canon Index

Purpose: preserve the major architectural laws, creative systems, generation contracts, verification rules, and development principles captured for DreamMusicForge / APEX Movie Forge so they are not dependent on chat history alone.

## 1. Product intent

DreamMusicForge / APEX Movie Forge is a provider-neutral cinematic intelligence system whose purpose is to transform human dream/intent into a verified finished cinematic experience.

The system must not degenerate into a sophisticated prompt box.

Canonical hierarchy:

HUMAN DREAM / INTENT
-> APEX MOVIE FORGE
-> Narrative Intelligence
-> Character Intelligence
-> World Intelligence
-> Performance Intelligence
-> Cinematography Intelligence
-> Music / Sound Intelligence
-> Editorial Intelligence
-> CANONICAL MOVIE STATE
-> Scene Contracts
-> Shot / Sequence Contracts
-> Provider Intelligence Layer
-> Provider Generation Contract / Packet
-> Renderer (Kling, Runway, etc.)
-> Actual Generated Media
-> APEX Inspection
-> Intent vs Actual
-> PASS -> Movie Assembly
-> FAIL -> Root-Cause Diagnosis -> Targeted Repair -> Recompile -> Regenerate

Core separation:

- APEX owns cinematic intelligence.
- The renderer owns media synthesis/rendering.
- Generated output may not rewrite canonical intent or world truth merely because the provider drifted.

## 3. Emotional architecture and editorial law

Walter Murch Rule of Six is canonical and frozen for DreamMusicForge.

Every cut, transition, shot change, camera reframe, audio transition, symbolic reveal, and generated-scene handoff is evaluated in this order:

1. Emotion - 51%
2. Story - 23%
3. Rhythm - 10%
4. Eye Trace - 7%
5. Two-Dimensional Plane of Screen - 5%
6. Three-Dimensional Space - 4%

When tradeoffs are unavoidable, sacrifice from the bottom upward.

Technical continuity may never outrank emotion, story, and rhythm merely because it is easier to measure.

## 4. Principle of Complete Lineage

No visible behavior, dialogue, camera movement, lighting change, edit, sound, musical cue, prop, costume choice, environment change, or symbolic element may exist without traceable causal lineage back to an underlying human state, narrative purpose, or physical cause.

Likewise, no invisible human state may be introduced unless it ultimately produces at least one observable consequence.

This governs:

- human state;
- narrative state;
- performance;
- cinematography;
- edit decisions;
- music and sound;
- symbolism;
- generated artifacts.

## 6. World + Spatial Scene Graph

The Asset Graph defines what persistent things exist.

The World + Spatial Scene Graph defines:

- where they exist;
- what contains what;
- what connects;
- what can see what;
- how entities are oriented;
- what spatial truths must survive across 2-D, 3-D, video, AR/VR, and simulation.

Formal separation:

WORLD - what reality/rule-set?
-> SPACE - what region?
-> SCENE STATE - what occupies it now?
-> VIEW / PROJECTION - how is it represented?

Canonical provider-neutral spatial convention:

- right-handed coordinate system;
- X horizontal;
- Y depth;
- Z vertical;
- meters and radians;
- explicit precision states: EXACT, BOUNDED, RELATIVE, APPROXIMATE, TOPOLOGICAL_ONLY, UNKNOWN.

The graph owns worlds, spaces, transforms, bounds, containment, topology, portals, occupancy, navigation, visibility, line-of-sight, spatial constraints, world evolution, cross-world transitions, and planar/3-D/cinematic/interactive projections.

Provider coordinate systems and generated spatial drift may not rewrite canonical spatial truth.

## 7. Scene, shot, and sequence contracts

Canonical movie state must compile into bounded execution contracts.

A Scene Contract should define the dramatic function, participants, environment, emotional target, performance state, world state, music/sound state, and success criteria.

A Shot / Sequence Contract should define the cinematic execution necessary to fulfill that scene intent, including:

- subject state;
- action;
- camera/lens/movement;
- composition;
- lighting;
- performance;
- dialogue / lyrics;
- sound;
- timing;
- continuity dependencies;
- transition strategy;
- verification criteria.

## 8. Provider Generation Contract

The Provider Generation Contract is the formal boundary between Movie Forge intelligence and a renderer such as Kling.

It should compile canonical intent into provider-specific packets without allowing provider-specific limitations to contaminate the canonical model.

Provider packets may include:

- references / identity anchors;
- frame or image anchors;
- camera direction;
- action and performance instructions;
- dialogue or singing instructions;
- sound requirements;
- duration and aspect ratio;
- start/end state requirements;
- continuity boundary requirements;
- provider-specific controls and known limitations.

Truth rule: a provider packet being constructed or dispatched does not prove generation success or quality.

## 9. Continuous video architecture

Long-form continuity is governed by `CONTINUOUS_VIDEO_CANON.md`.

Canonical classes:

- TRUE_CONTINUOUS_SPAN;
- CONCEALED_CONTINUITY_SPAN;
- MOTIVATED_SCENE_TRANSITION.

Every spanning boundary should be governed by a Boundary Contract that may include character state, motion phase, camera state, world state, transition-device state, coverage targets, allowed state changes, and next-segment start requirements.

Continuity-safe zones may deliberately use near-total/total occlusion to hide otherwise difficult state resets.

Important retained conclusion from the August 28, 2026 experiment:

The scalable target is not necessarily six perfect 15-second continuations. It is one coherent 90-second audience experience assembled from the best continuity strategy at each boundary.

## 10. Audio and music architecture

Music and sound are first-class cinematic intelligence layers, not incidental output from the video renderer.

For long-form segmented visual generation, maintain one continuous master audio timeline for:

- music;
- dialogue;
- narration;
- ambience;
- Foley;
- footsteps;
- sound effects;
- designed transitions.

Provider-generated audio may be harvested selectively but must not automatically own or reset the master timeline.

Retained voice/mood guidance from earlier DreamMusicForge work:

- soft reflective narrator when appropriate;
- warm energy;
- music progression example: ambient cinematic piano -> soaring orchestral anthem.

## 11. Singing, dialogue, and lip-sync

Dialogue, singing, lyrics, voice identity, phoneme timing, semantic correctness, and lip-sync are separate verification dimensions.

Do not collapse them into one generic "audio passed" status.

A visually plausible mouth movement does not prove:

- the correct words were sung;
- correct phoneme timing;
- correct voice identity;
- correct emotional performance;
- synchronization across a long-form edit.

These require independent evidence and repair loops.

## 12. Inspection and repair

Every generated artifact should be inspected against the contract that caused it.

Inspection categories include:

- character identity;
- wardrobe/hair/makeup;
- world/spatial state;
- story intent;
- emotional target;
- performance;
- camera and composition;
- lighting;
- temporal/motion continuity;
- transition quality;
- dialogue/singing;
- lip-sync;
- sound/music continuity;
- editing rhythm;
- symbolic/narrative lineage.

Failure handling:

FAIL
-> root-cause diagnosis
-> identify causal contract/state failure
-> targeted repair only
-> recompile affected packet
-> regenerate affected scope
-> reinspect

Avoid broad regeneration when a narrow repair can preserve already-verified work.

## 13. Evidence, truth, and correctness kernel

APEX Principle of Best-Effort Correctness / APEX 9N-infinity Truth-and-Correctness Kernel is top-level doctrine.

Optimize for verified convergence from human intent to independently verified reality, not confidence, activity, proxy scores, or appearance of correctness.

Distinguish:

- continuous quality error where meaningful;
- probabilistic reliability claims only when scoped and evidenced;
- hard invariants with zero tolerated critical violations.

Nine Truth Invariants:

1. no guarantee without valid proof;
2. missing evidence cannot produce proof status;
3. invalid dependencies invalidate dependent guarantees;
4. claims cannot escape their verified validity domain;
5. verifier uncertainty cannot default to PASS;
6. critical contradictions block guaranteed completion;
7. critical unknowns block absolute completion;
8. claim strength may never exceed evidence strength;
9. absence of detected error is not proof of absence of error.

For media generation, separately track provider dispatch, provider completion, identity, continuity, spatial truth, lip-sync, lyric/dialogue correctness, audio continuity, transition invisibility, and final creative quality.

It is not evidence for:

This distinction is mandatory in status reports and release evidence.

Simulation is a development accelerator and evidence tool, not a substitute for provider or audience proof.

Use simulation to test:

- scene structure;
- continuity architecture;

## 17. Renderer strategy

Kling is a renderer, not the governing intelligence layer.

Runway, HeyGen, Remotion, and other media systems may be integrated where useful, but they should enter through explicit provider/tool contracts and evidence boundaries rather than becoming architectural dependencies of the canonical movie model.

The architecture should remain provider-neutral even when a specific provider is currently preferred for a use case.

## 18. OUP / observable usability principle applied to media tooling

APEX Observable Usability Precedence remains relevant to production tooling and review interfaces.

For a media production/review workflow, lower-level usability must be satisfied before feature richness:

- usable workspace;
- readable information;
- responsive controls;
- understandable outcomes;
- task difficulty;
- feature richness.

A sophisticated generator that cannot be inspected, compared, repaired, and understood is not a successful production system.

## 19. GitHub development discipline

Every verified build/pass or material evidence-producing increment should be committed to GitHub so each step has:

- version history;
- evidence;
- tests where applicable;
- rollback point;
- truth boundary.

Development should proceed in small, independently testable and releasable increments rather than large monolithic builds.

Do not claim a remote commit, branch, push, PR, CI pass, release, or merge unless that operation is actually verified.
