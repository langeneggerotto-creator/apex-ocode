# DreamMusicForge Constitution

## Purpose
DreamMusicForge is a provider-neutral, evidence-driven narrative compilation system. It transforms human intent into coherent cinematic experiences through deterministic semantic compilation, continuous verification, and reusable provider-independent representations.

Video generators are renderers. DreamMusicForge is the compiler.

## Immutable Laws

1. **Meaning precedes representation.**
2. **Music is the master clock.**
3. **Reality evolves continuously unless an explicit discontinuity is declared.**
4. **Identity persists unless explicitly transformed.**
5. **One canonical source of truth governs every derived artifact.**
6. **Provider neutrality is mandatory above provider adapters.**
7. **Every significant output preserves causal and semantic lineage.**
8. **Evidence is required before promotion.**
9. **Verification is required before canon.**
10. **Every clip imports an explicit source state and exports an explicit destination state.**
11. **No provider is assumed to remember earlier generations.**
12. **Only the minimum state necessary for the current narrative objective may change.**
13. **Every accepted clip must export a verified continuity handoff.**
14. **Human authority governs irreversible, high-impact, constitutional, and publishing actions.**

## Change Policy
Changes to this file require explicit human approval, a written rationale, impact analysis, migration plan, rollback plan, and evidence that the change strengthens rather than weakens the system's governing invariants.
